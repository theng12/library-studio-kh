# What's new in Library Studio KH

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-05

- First version: one list of what's downloading, what's in your library and what's coming up.
- Works with your own qBittorrent, Sonarr, Radarr, Lidarr and Prowlarr. Add their addresses in Settings.
- Local copies are removed 7 days after they reach the library. You can change the number of days or switch this off.
- A download is only removed after Sonarr, Radarr or Lidarr confirms it is fully in the library. Anything else stays.
- Shows the free space left on this computer and the cleanup settings it puts in qBittorrent and the other apps.
- Browse, search and sort everything in your library, and find new shows, films and artists to add.
- Paste magnet or web links, or choose .torrent files, one or many at a time, and they go to qBittorrent.
- Click a download to see when it started and finished, how long it took, its size, speeds and share ratio.
- Records download speed every 15 seconds while the app is open, to show the lowest, fastest and time at each speed.
